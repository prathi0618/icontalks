What is the duration of the course? ▼

Our Professional program is 16 weeks (4 months) with live interactive sessions. You get lifetime access to all recorded content, so you can learn at your own pace even after the program ends.

Do I need prior programming experience? ▼

Not at all! We teach Java from scratch and cover all basics. Our curriculum is designed for beginners to advanced learners. However, some basic computer knowledge is helpful.

What's the job placement guarantee? ▼

We provide 100% placement assistance through our network of 500+ partner companies. Our team helps with resume building, interview prep, and job interviews. 95% of our students get placed within 3 months.

What's the average salary after the course? ▼

Our students receive an average salary of 6.8 LPA (Lakhs Per Annum) in their first role. Many report a 40-60% salary increase if they were already working in manual QA.

Can I access the course materials after completion? ▼

Yes! You get lifetime access to all video lectures, notes, and code files. You can revisit the material anytime for reference or to refresh your knowledge.

How are the live sessions structured? ▼

Classes are held 3-4 times per week for 2-3 hours each. Sessions include theory, live coding, Q&A, and assignments. All sessions are recorded for those who can't attend live.

What if I don't get placed after the course? ▼

Our placement team continues to support you with interview practice, resume updates, and job matching. If placement isn't guaranteed in your plan, we offer a satisfaction guarantee or money-back option.

Yes! Our certificate is recognized by 500+ partner companies and major IT firms. More importantly, your practical skills and portfolio projects demonstrate your competency to employers.
